The thing nobody says out loud

Nobody wastes their life.
They let it drift.

An hour on the phone. Two in front of the TV. A whole evening that went nowhere. None of it chosen, none of it remembered. Each hour feels like nothing. Add them up.

How it works

Nothing you do is loose.

⏱ Session→ Habit or task→ Goal→ Pillar

Every hour lands on a habit or a task, which moves a goal, which builds a pillar of your life. Nothing floats.

1

Choose your pillars.

Very few things actually matter. Name them: your body, your mind, your money, your craft. Everything else is noise, and Intentional treats it that way.

  • 3 to 5 pillars, chosen by you
  • One goal in each, with a deadline you can see coming
  • “On track”, “ahead” or “behind”, never red
2

Give your hours to them.

Start a session on a habit or a task. Flick burns with you while the hour is protected, and every minute is written to the goal and the pillar it built.

  • Block the apps you choose while you work
  • Missed your 7:00? “Do it now”, or take a rest day
  • Streaks that forgive one skip a week
3

Face your week.

Every Sunday evening, three honest minutes: the hours each pillar really got, the habits you kept, your best day. Then decide what next week is for.

  • One accountability partner gets your week
  • If you go quiet, they ask why
4

Watch your life add up.

A record that only grows: every hour you’ve ever given to the life you chose, month by month, with the goals you finished and what they took. The flame grows with it, from Spark to Sun.
