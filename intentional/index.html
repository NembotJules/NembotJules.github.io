How it works

Nothing you do is loose.

⏱ Session→ Habit or task→ Goal→ Pillar

Every focus session lands on a habit or a task, which moves a goal, which serves a pillar of your life. At the end of the week you can see exactly where your life went.

Pillars & goals

Very few things actually matter.

Pick the 3 to 5 areas of your life that do. Give each one goal you can finish, and see how many hours it really got this week.

  • Physique, Mind, Finances, Skills, or your own
  • “On track”, “ahead” or “behind” for every goal, without the red
  • Habits that are part of a goal, not a checklist of their own

Focus

Start the hour. Flick burns with you.

Start a session on a habit or a task. The phone goes quiet, the timer runs, and every minute is counted toward the goal and the pillar it serves.

  • Timer-only or strict mode
  • Missed your 7:00? “Do it now” or take a rest day
  • Streaks that forgive one skip a week

Sunday

Your week, on one card.

Every Sunday evening, a three-minute look back: hours per pillar, habits kept, your best day, your goal’s progress. Then plan the next week.

  • One accountability partner gets your week, by message
  • A card worth sharing, in light or dark
  • No feed, no followers, no likes

The long game

Your life, in hours.

A ledger that only grows. Every hour you’ve given to the life you chose, month by month and pillar by pillar, with the goals you achieved and what they took.

  • Flick grows from Spark to Sun as your hours add up
  • Your data stays on your phone

No guilt

Missed a habit? Do it now or rest today. Nothing turns red, and nothing tells you that you failed.

One partner, not a feed

One person who sees your week and asks why when you go quiet. That’s the whole social network.

Yours, on your phone

Your hours, goals and notes live on your device. No account needed to start.
